Live red-spot detection · approximate practice scoring · runs in your browser

1. Camera

Ready. Start the camera and point it at your paper target.

Tip: keep the phone steady, aim the camera at the target, avoid bright red objects in the frame, and keep the target well lit. Detection is experimental.

2. Calibrate the target area

Tap the target area’s top-left corner in the camera preview, then tap its bottom-right corner. Keep the camera facing the target as square-on as possible. Calibration maps the selected rectangle to the scoring target.

Camera must be running to calibrate.

Safety and limitations

Use only a low-power laser pointer on a paper target. Never aim a laser at eyes, people, animals, mirrors, vehicles, or the camera lens. Do not attach the pointer to a real firearm or airgun. This is a home practice prototype, not an official scoring system.
