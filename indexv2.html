Laser Shooting Trainer

Version 2 · Camera visibility test + manual scoring

Laser safety: Point the laser only at a paper target, never at anyone's eyes, reflective surfaces, or the phone camera lens. Keep the camera pointed at the target. Do not attach the pointer to a firearm or airgun. Use a low-power pointer and stop if the spot is not clearly visible.

1. Camera test

Mount the phone so it sees the target. Allow camera access, then point your laser at the paper target from the side of the phone. Tap “Analyse frame” to check for a red spot. Detection is experimental and may be affected by lighting or red printing.
